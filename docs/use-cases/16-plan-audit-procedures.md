# UC-16 — Plan and Review Audit Procedures

**Actor:** Engagement Partner and Audit Staff. **Goal:** Track planned audit objectives, evidence requirements, human judgments and review completion beyond rule-generated findings.

## Preconditions

The engagement has a defined client, period, scope, jurisdiction and assigned reviewer.

## Main success flow

1. Auditor records relevant financial statement risks/assertions and applicable methodology.
2. Partner or authorized reviewer approves materiality assumptions and required procedures.
3. Each procedure specifies required data, coverage expectations, preparer/reviewer, evidence and conclusion.
4. Auditor performs or documents a manual/automated procedure; system records versioned workpaper and result.
5. Reviewer documents comments, requested amendments, approval or limitation.
6. Sign-off checks procedure status and current dataset versions, not merely absence of Critical findings.

## Failure and alternative flows

- Mandatory procedure unperformed → block ordinary sign-off.
- Missing or unreconciled supporting data → mark limitation; no false 'completed' state.
- Changed source after review → dependent procedure becomes stale and requires reevaluation.
- Auditor and reviewer are the same actor in a workflow requiring separation → reject approval.

## Acceptance criteria

Every required procedure has an accountable actor, data dependencies, evidence and reviewer decision; missing work and stale results are explicit blockers.
